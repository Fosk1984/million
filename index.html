    How to play

    Answer 15 ascending-difficulty questions on shoulder pathology. You have one use each of 50:50, Ask the Crowd, and Ask an Expert. Safe havens are at £1,000 (Q5) and £32,000 (Q10). Timer is 45s/question (toggle in Teacher panel).

    • 50:50: removes two incorrect options.
    • Ask the Crowd: shows a simulated poll (less reliable on harder questions).
    • Ask an Expert: shows a hint with ~75% reliability (drops on the hardest items).
